1. EXECUTIVE BUSINESS OVERVIEW & RETAIL ECONOMICS
================================================================================

1.1 THE CLIENT & PRODUCT CONTEXT:
The Sleep Company is an omnichannel direct-to-consumer (D2C) brand known for patented SmartGrid mattresses, ergonomic chairs, and smart recliners (Elev8). Their average basket size ranges from ₹30,000 to ₹75,000+. 

1.2 THE BUSINESS PROBLEM (THE RETAIL "WALKOUT LEAK"):
- In offline retail stores (like Indiranagar and HSR in Bangalore), high-intent shoppers spend 20 to 40 minutes testing mattresses, asking about EMI, and comparing firmness.
- Bain (2024) retail studies show that 86% of retail walkouts leave with zero structured follow-up. Store staff are busy attending new walk-ins and fail to capture customer details or follow up before the lead goes cold.
- When an undecided customer leaves the store, they frequently end up buying from a competitor (Wakefit, Duroflex, Sleepwell) within 24 to 48 hours.
- A single walkout represents ₹30,000 to ₹75,000 in lost gross merchandise value (GMV).

1.3 SPEED-TO-LEAD & CONVERSION METRICS:
- On-Ground.ai's benchmark data demonstrates a +23.6 percentage point increase in conversion when an agent calls the shopper within 30 to 60 minutes of leaving the store and specifically names the customer, store, and mattress model.
- Store revenue experiences a measured +2% overall lift when walkout recovery is automated.

1.4 THE DUAL CALLING PHILOSOPHY IN THIS PORTAL:
Why does the portal have two distinct calling modes?
1) IN-APP BROWSER CALL ("The AI Pitch Coach"):
   - Target User: The Call Centre Executive / Human Employee (Arvind Rajan).
   - Purpose: Zero-cost sales pitch training and objection handling. New agents can practice handling tough customer objections (e.g., price resistance, EMI inquiries, warranty doubts) against an intelligent AI customer before dialing high-value real leads.
   - Value: Ensures agents never burn a ₹50,000 prospect due to poor pitch execution.

2) PHYSICAL PHONE CALL ("The Outbound Recovery Engine"):
   - Target User: The Real Walkout Customer (e.g., Mukunda).
   - Purpose: Automated carrier telephony (PSTN) that dials the shopper's mobile phone to deliver immediate walkout recovery offers (25% weekend voucher, zero-cost EMI terms, WhatsApp quotation link).
   - Value: Scalability. 100 walkouts across 10 stores can be engaged in minutes without requiring 10 additional telecallers.

          │    └── Synthesizes localized Indian-English audio (voice: alice)
          │
          └──> Call Polling & Remote Hangup Service
               └── Syncs call state (ringing -> in-progress -> completed)

2.2 SYSTEM DATA FLOW LIFECYCLE:
1. Signal Capture: Walkout event captured from Indiranagar POS tablet. Lead created with ICP Fit (87%) and Intent Score (92%).
2. Prioritization: Plan engine ranks leads on the agent's dashboard by deal value and urgency.
3. Warm-up (Optional): Agent launches "Practise a call" in browser to warm up and test pitch scoring against AI objections.
4. Call Execution: Agent clicks "Phone Call" or "Call back". Express triggers Twilio outbound API.

4. IN-APP CALLING ENGINE (BROWSER AI PITCH COACH)
================================================================================

File: `frontend/src/components/CallModal.jsx`

4.1 HOW IT WORKS:
The In-App Call is an interactive voice roleplay environment where the human agent sits at the computer, speaks into their microphone, and an AI plays the role of a skeptical retail customer (Mukunda) to test the agent's sales skills.

4.2 UNDER THE HOOD: SPEECH RECOGNITION & SYNTHESIS:
- Speech Recognition (STT): Uses browser-native `window.SpeechRecognition || window.webkitSpeechRecognition`.
- Speech Synthesis (TTS): Uses `window.speechSynthesis` and `SpeechSynthesisUtterance` with an Indian-English accent profile (`en-IN`).

4.3 CRITICAL ENGINEERING BUG FIX (Chromium `InvalidStateError`):
- Technical Bug: In Google Chrome, calling `.start()` on an existing `SpeechRecognition` instance that previously stopped or errored throws an uncatchable `DOMException: Failed to execute 'start' on 'SpeechRecognition': recognition has already started`.
- Solution Implemented: On every recording activation, the component creates a **brand-new `SpeechRecognition` instance**, registers fresh `.onresult`, `.onerror`, and `.onend` event handlers, and dispatches it cleanly. This ensures the microphone never locks up or goes silent during a session.

4.4 DUAL VOICE INPUT MODES:
1) Click-to-Speak Toggle: Click once to turn the microphone on (button pulses red with a live speech bubble showing real-time transcription). Click again when finished speaking to submit.
2) Hold-to-Talk (Walkie-Talkie): Press and hold down the spacebar or mouse button to talk; release to submit immediately. Perfect for noisy call center floors.

4.5 INTELLIGENT NEGATION & CONVERSATIONAL EVALUATOR:
Naive keyword matching fails in sales evaluation. For example, if the agent says *"I will not send the quotation"*, a basic keyword trigger for `"send"` would falsely praise the agent.
Our engine uses an advanced **Negation-Aware Parser**:
- Regex Negation Detection: `/\b(not|won't|cannot|no|never|refuse|disagree)\b/i`.
- If an agent says *"Yeah sure I will not send"* or *"I won't give discount"*:
  1. The engine detects the negation qualifier immediately.
  2. The customer responds with realistic pushback: *"Wait, why won't you send the quotation? How can I review the price without seeing it?"*
  3. The agent's Objection Handling score drops by -10% with an amber warning badge.
- Off-Topic / Gibberish Detection: If the agent says something unrelated (e.g., *"I am iron"* or random noise):
  1. Customer responds with confusion: *"Sorry, I didn't quite catch that. How does that relate to the SmartGrid mattress I tested in Indiranagar?"*
  2. The conversation does not falsely advance, and the score remains uncredited.
- Stock & EMI Objection Handling:
  - If agent explains 6-month zero-cost EMI on Bajaj Finserv: Score jumps by +15%.
  - If agent confirms stock at Indiranagar: Score jumps by +10%.
  - Once the pitch achieves valid qualification, the AI agrees: *"Okay great, please send the official quote and payment link to my WhatsApp."*

5. TELEPHONY ENGINE (TWILIO PSTN INTEGRATION)
================================================================================

Files: `frontend/src/components/PhoneCallModal.jsx` & `calling-backend/server.js`

5.1 HOW THE PHONE CALL WORKS:
When an agent clicks "Phone call", the portal triggers an outbound telephony sequence via Twilio Voice API to reach real mobile phone numbers over the cellular carrier network (PSTN).

5.2 AGENT-FIRST DIALING ARCHITECTURE:
In enterprise telephony, customers are never dialed first to wait on dead air. The system employs an Agent-First dispatch:
1. Frontend sends `POST /api/call` with payload: `{ to: "+919876543210", customerName: "Mukunda", product: "SmartGRID Ortho King", store: "Indiranagar" }`.
2. Backend calls `twilioClient.calls.create` specifying dynamic TwiML instructions.
3. Twilio dials the configured phone number and executes the call lifecycle.

5.3 TWIML GENERATION LIFECYCLE:
```javascript
const twimlResponse = new twilio.twiml.VoiceResponse();
// 1. Audio Bearer Warmup: 2-second pause ensures carrier audio is open after pickup
twimlResponse.pause({ length: 2 });

// 2. Localized Audio Greeting
twimlResponse.say(
  { voice: 'alice', language: 'en-IN' },
  `Hello ${name}! This is Sarah calling from The Sleep Company ${loc} regarding your inquiry for the ${prod}. How can I help you today?`
);

// 3. Speech Recognition Listener on Customer Phone
const gather = twimlResponse.gather({
  input: 'speech',
  action: actionUrl,
  language: 'en-IN',
  speechTimeout: 'auto',
  timeout: 4
});
gather.say({ voice: 'alice', language: 'en-IN' }, 'Go ahead, I am listening.');

// 4. Default Offer Fallback
twimlResponse.say(
  { voice: 'alice', language: 'en-IN' },
  `I am happy to let you know that the ${prod} is in stock, and our 6-month zero-cost EMI on Bajaj Finserv is available today. We will send the quotation link directly to your WhatsApp.`
);
```

5.4 REAL-TIME STATUS POLLING & AUTO-DISMISSAL:
- Once dialed, the backend returns the Twilio `callSid` (e.g., `CA3f7b...`).
- `PhoneCallModal.jsx` mounts and polls `GET /api/call-status/:callSid` every 1,500ms.
- As the call moves through Twilio statuses (`queued` -> `ringing` -> `in-progress` -> `completed`):
  - When in-progress: UI shows live connected status and ticks duration timer (`00:15`, `00:16`).
  - When customer hangs up mobile phone: Twilio reports status `completed`.
  - Polling interval catches `completed`, captures final billable duration, displays "Call ended (completed)", and **automatically closes the modal after 1.5 seconds**.
- Remote Hangup: Clicking "End Phone Call" sends `POST /api/call/hangup`, which calls `twilioClient.calls(callSid).update({ status: 'completed' })` to immediately terminate the carrier connection.

6. BACKEND & CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE
================================================================================

File: `calling-backend/server.js`

6.1 ENVIRONMENT CONFIGURATION:
- Safe multi-environment dotenv loader:
  ```javascript
  require('dotenv').config();
  require('dotenv').config({ path: path.resolve(__dirname, '../.env') });
  ```
  This guarantees that environment variables are loaded regardless of whether the app is started from the repository root, the subfolder, or cloud hosting (Render).

6.2 REST API ENDPOINTS:
- `GET /api/health`: Health check endpoint verifying Twilio configuration without exposing secret tokens.
- `POST /api/call`: Initiates outbound Twilio voice call. Normalizes phone numbers with regex formatting.
- `GET /api/call-status/:callSid`: Fetches live status, duration, and metadata for an active call.
- `POST /api/call/hangup`: Remotely terminates an active Twilio call.
- `POST /api/twilio/voice-reply`: Twilio webhook that evaluates caller speech and dynamically replies back with contextual TwiML.

6.3 CLOUD WEBHOOK RESOLUTION:
```javascript
const baseUrl = process.env.PUBLIC_URL || process.env.RENDER_EXTERNAL_URL || '';
const actionUrl = baseUrl ? `${baseUrl}/api/twilio/voice-reply` : '/api/twilio/voice-reply';
```
When deployed on Render.com, `process.env.RENDER_EXTERNAL_URL` is automatically populated by Render with the live HTTPS domain (e.g., `https://onground-backend.onrender.com`). This allows Twilio to immediately reach the webhook without requiring any manual ngrok tunnels.

7. TECHNICAL INTERVIEW DEFENSE & CORE QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q1: Why did you choose React + Vite instead of Next.js or Create React App?
Answer:
- The brief asked to build the internal Call Centre portal that an agent keeps open on their laptop throughout an 8-hour shift. This is an authenticated, internal Single Page Application (SPA), not a public e-commerce store requiring Server-Side Rendering (SSR) for search engine indexing.
- Vite 5 provides near-instant Hot Module Replacement (HMR) powered by native ES modules in development, and generates an optimized production Rollup bundle built in ~3.6 seconds.
- Create React App (CRA) is deprecated and unmaintained, while Next.js introduces unnecessary SSR overhead and serverless cold starts for an operational agent dashboard.

Q2: How did you implement voice calling in the browser, and what technical hurdles did you overcome?
Answer:
- In the browser, I implemented an AI Pitch Coach using the native Web Speech API (`SpeechRecognition` for audio transcription and `SpeechSynthesis` for customer voice playback).
- The biggest technical challenge was Google Chrome's `InvalidStateError`: in Chromium browsers, calling `.start()` on a stopped SpeechRecognition instance crashes silently or throws an exception.
- I architected the speech hook to discard and instantiate a fresh `SpeechRecognition` instance on every single utterance.
- Additionally, naive keyword matching causes severe false positives (e.g., praising the agent when they say *"I will NOT send the quote"*). I implemented a regex-based negation parser that catches negative qualifiers (`not`, `won't`, `cannot`) and triggers realistic customer pushback while decreasing the pitch score.

Q3: How does the telephony integration work with Twilio, and how do you handle local development without ngrok?
Answer:
- For real phone calls, the backend uses the Twilio Node.js SDK to initiate outbound PSTN calls using inline TwiML strings.
- Because the TwiML is passed directly in the REST API payload to Twilio (`twiml: twimlResponse.toString()`), Twilio executes the text-to-speech greeting directly inside its cloud telecom infrastructure without needing to request a webhook from localhost. This completely eliminates the need for third-party tunnels like ngrok during local development.
- For production, the server detects Render's automatic `RENDER_EXTERNAL_URL` and routes bi-directional speech recognition callbacks to `/api/twilio/voice-reply`.

Q4: How is state managed across the application, and how does the dashboard stay reactive?
Answer:
- Global operational state is managed at the `App.jsx` level using React hooks (`useState`, `useEffect`) and passed down through clean props.
- When an agent completes a call or checks a task, the central mutation function `markLeadAndTaskCompleted` executes in constant time:
  1. It maps over the `tasks` array and marks the matching task as completed with a timestamp (`Called just now`).
  2. It increments `callsPlacedCount` and updates the shift quota counter (`18 / 25` -> `20 / 25`).
  3. It updates the "Who's Waiting" queue and changes the lead's status to `Contacted`.
- This ensures high UI reactivity without the memory overhead of complex global stores like Redux.

Q5: What security practices did you implement for API keys and secrets?
Answer:
- All sensitive credentials (`TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`, `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN`, `TWILIO_NUMBER`, `AGENT_PHONE`) are strictly stored in root `.env` files and excluded from Git using `.gitignore`.
- The `/api/health` endpoint sanitizes output by returning masked strings (e.g., `+141...` and `+9198...`) and boolean flags so that backend health can be validated without exposing raw credentials in browser network tabs.

Q6: How would you scale this architecture to 500 retail stores across India?
Answer:
1. WebSockets / PubSub: Replace polling with a lightweight WebSocket server (or Redis Pub/Sub) so that when a walkout occurs at Indiranagar Store #14, the task pops up on Arvind's screen in real time with zero latency.
2. WebRTC / Twilio Voice SDK: Transition the browser calling from Web Speech API to Twilio Voice Client WebRTC so that agents wearing USB headsets can talk directly through the browser to the customer's phone over a SIP trunk.
3. Database Tier: Move from in-memory mock data to PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM, partitioning leads by store ID and agent shift schedules.
